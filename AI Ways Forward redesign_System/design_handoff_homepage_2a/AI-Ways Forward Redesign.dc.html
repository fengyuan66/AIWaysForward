1c Design system — drawn from the current site
AI-Ways Forward

The existing identity already has three strong signals: a high-contrast serif wordmark, a hand-drawn red arrow, and hard black/brick-red fields. The system below keeps those and adds the structure the current site lacks — a type scale, spacing, and component rules.

Color
Ink#141414
Charcoal#3C3936
Arrow Red#A33328
Deep Brick#6E2622
Paper#F1EFEC
Rule#E0DCD5
Type
Display serif Playfair Display 600 · wordmark, H1
Heading sans Sora 800 · H2, H3
Body copy sits at 17–20px with 1.55 line height and a 70ch measure. Space Grotesk 400
Eyebrow / label Sora 500 · 0.16em
Components
Primary → Primary, square Secondary Text link →
Arrow motif
Used once per screen, under the wordmark or a hero headline. Never as a divider.
Spacing 4 · 8 · 12 · 20 · 32 · 48 · 72 px
Gallery art to source

Tiles should read as one set: real people and real work, shot in daylight, then treated with a duotone of Ink and Arrow Red at roughly 60% strength so the rail holds together. Priority shots, in order: students working at laptops in a classroom; a professional using AI on the job (surgeon, architect, or engineer); a hackathon table; a speaker in front of a room; a close macro of an AI interface; and the existing abstract node render, which stays as the one non-photographic tile.
